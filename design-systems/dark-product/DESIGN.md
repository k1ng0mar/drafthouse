# Dark Product

App/marketing dark UI — neutral charcoal, not purple cyberpunk.

## Type
- Display: Georgia / Fraunces serif for marketing H1
- UI: system-ui

## Color
- Canvas `#0c0a09`, elevated `#1c1917`
- Text `#e7e5e4`, muted `#a8a29e`
- Accent `--dk-accent` `#d97706` (amber) — not indigo/violet
- Success `--dk-ok` `#4d7c0f`

## Rules
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body on canvas
- Borders subtle `#292524`
- One accent; amber for CTA
- Avoid neon glows and gradient heroes unless brief demands
