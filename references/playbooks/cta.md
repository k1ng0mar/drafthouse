# Playbook: CTA

**Refs:** `cta-gallery` · `drafthouse refs search cta`

## Patterns
1. **Inline** after a section argument
2. **Band** full-width strip before footer
3. **Hero** primary next to H1

## Do
- Verb-first label (“Request lab access”, not “Submit”)
- One primary per viewport
- Hover + focus-visible

## Don’t
- “Click here”
- Fake scarcity (“Only 2 seats left!”) unless true
- Gradient buttons with emoji

## Structure checks
`has-cta`
