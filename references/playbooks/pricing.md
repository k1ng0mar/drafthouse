# Playbook: Pricing

**Refs:** `pricing-page`, `saaspo` · `drafthouse refs search pricing`

## IA

```
H1 + honest qualifier
[plan card] × 3  — name, price or “on request”, 3–6 bullets, CTA
fine print: what usage limits exist (no invented SLAs)
```

## Do
- Align feature rows where possible
- “Talk to us” for custom tiers
- Highlight one recommended plan via border/label, not neon glow

## Don’t
- Fake “Most popular” without basis
- Invented discounts or countdown timers
- Dense 20-row comparison on v1 plates

## Structure checks
`has-pricing`, `has-cta`
